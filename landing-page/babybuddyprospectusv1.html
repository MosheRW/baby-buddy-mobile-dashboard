What it does

Small enough for one hand, honest enough for two parents.

No dashboards to configure, no forms with fields you'll never use. Just what actually happens in a day with a baby.

Everything in one timeline

Diapers, feeds, sleep, medicine, temperature, tummy time — one feed per child, in the order it actually happened.

Timers that don't lose their place

Start a feed or a nap and walk away. It keeps counting on your lock screen and picks up right where you left it.

Room for every caregiver

Partner, grandparent, sitter — everyone logs to the same server. A quiet weekly recap shows who did what.

Reminders that check twice

Before a notification buzzes you, it re-checks the server. If someone else already handled it, yours stays quiet.

Medicine, done carefully

Doses, units, and 24-hour limits, with a plain-language read on when the next one's due — or overdue.

Feels like it's yours

Light or dark, English or עברית — right to left, right where it should be. On newer phones, it even borrows your wallpaper's color.

A day, roughly

What using it actually looks like.

2:14AM

The bottle timer's already running from the last feed. You log the ounces half-asleep, without a single screen that asks you something it doesn't need to.

7:40AM

Grandma opens the same server on her own phone. She sees the whole night — every wake, every change — without anyone having to summarize it for her.

1:30PM

Naptime. Tummy time gets its eight minutes logged with a tap, and the medicine card quietly counts down to the next dose in the background.

SunWEEKLY

A short recap of who logged what this week lands once. It's not a scoreboard — it's just nice to see the week laid out honestly.

Why self-hosted

Your kid's data lives on your server. Full stop.

Baby Buddy Dashboard is a phone app for Baby Buddy, the open-source tracker you already run yourself — on your own hardware, or as a Home Assistant add-on. There's no separate account, no company reselling sleep patterns, no cloud copy of your baby's schedule sitting on someone else's server.

  • No subscription. Point the app at your server and sign in — that's the whole setup.
  • No stranger reads it. Every request goes straight to the server you control.
  • Nothing lost if we disappear. Baby Buddy's data outlives any one app built on top of it.
