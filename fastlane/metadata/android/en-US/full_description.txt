A warm, fast dashboard for logging your baby's day — diapers, feedings, medications, temperature, tummy time, and sleep — for one child or several.

Connect it to your own Baby Buddy server, run it through the Home Assistant add-on, or use it completely offline on your phone with no server at all. Your data stays yours.

Note: this is an independent, third-party client for a Baby Buddy server. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Baby Buddy project.

LOG EVERYTHING IN SECONDS
One tap on the dashboard opens a form already set to the right child and type:
• Diaper — pee and poo tracked separately, with colour and consistency
• Feeding — breast, bottle, or solids, by amount or by duration
• Medication — dose, unit, route, and scheduling
• Temperature, tummy time, sleep, and free-text notes

SEE "HOW LONG SINCE…" AT A GLANCE
Each child's card shows the time since the last diaper, feed, and sleep, updating on its own — alongside a 24-hour food summary and a food-trend bar.

STAY ON TOP OF MEDICATIONS
The app tells you when a dose is due, overdue, or allowed again, tracks 24-hour limits, and lets you log a repeat dose with a single tap.

TIMERS THAT DON'T STOP WHEN YOU CLOSE THE APP
Start a feeding, sleep, or tummy-time timer and finish it later — it turns into a ready-to-save entry, with a live countdown in your notification shade.

REMINDERS THAT STAY HONEST
Medication-due nudges, forgotten-timer alerts, diaper-interval and minimum-feed reminders, a live ticking countdown to the next dose, and a weekly summary. Every reminder is re-checked against the server before it shows, so it never nags you about something another caregiver already handled.

TAILOR IT TO YOUR FAMILY
Hide children, group them, give each a colour, and set schedules that quietly hide a child during set hours. Shake the phone to peek at hidden children.

MAKE IT YOURS TO LOOK AT
• Light, dark, or follow-the-system theming
• Optional Material You colour from your wallpaper (Android 12+)
• English or Hebrew (right-to-left), following your Baby Buddy profile by default

HOW YOU CONNECT
Choose one when you first sign in, and switch later by signing out:
• Baby Buddy — you host Baby Buddy yourself (server URL + username/password, an API key, or a QR login)
• Home Assistant — you run the Baby Buddy add-on (add-on URL + a long-lived access token)
• Offline — no server at all; just name your first child on the way in

PRIVACY & YOUR DATA
• Your password is never stored. It is used once to obtain an access token, which is then kept in your phone's secure storage.
• In offline mode, everything you log stays on your device — no account, no network.
• In the online modes, your data lives on your own server; the app only talks to the server you point it at.
